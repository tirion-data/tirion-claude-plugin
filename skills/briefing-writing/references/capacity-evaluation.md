# Template: capacity evaluation

A focused wealth assessment for one prospect: one to two pages. Research shops
use it to rate a pool for a unit's campaign, to refresh a stale rating, or to
answer "what could this person give" without a full profile. It ends with a
one-line capacity statement in the form the profession uses:

> Major gift capacity: <low> to <high> (tier), over 3-5 years, based on <indicators>.

The method behind the number is [capacity-research](../../capacity-research/SKILL.md).
This file is the document.

```markdown
# Capacity evaluation: <Full name>
**Confidential: for internal use by <organization> only**
Prepared <YYYY-MM-DD> by <researcher or team>, with AI assistance. Reviewed by <name>, <date>.
Requested by <officer>, for <unit or campaign>. Facts current as of <date>.

**<Full name>** (<degrees and years, if any; volunteer roles; parent status>), <city, state>.
Spouse or partner: <name; degrees or ties to us, if public> [n].
Identity confirmed by: <anchors: employer, city, filing>.

## Background
<One paragraph: current role and organization, what the organization does and
its size if public, career in two or three lines, corporate boards. Cite.>

## Wealth indicators
**Real estate.** <Each property: city and state, ownership vehicle, assessed
value and roll year, estimated market value and the basis for the adjustment,
purchase price and date if recorded. Record and date for each.> [n]

**Public-company stock.** <Shares held directly and indirectly as of the latest
Form 4 or proxy, value at a stated price and date, sales since <year> with
gross proceeds. "Last reported <date>; current holding not confirmed" for a
departed insider.> [n]

**Compensation.** <Salary, bonus, incentive and awards from the proxy, by year;
awards are grant-date values, not cash. For a partner or owner: the estimate
and the convention used.> [n]

**Private company and partnership interests.** <Signal, basis, source. Upside,
not a number, unless a filing gives one.> [n]

**Family foundation.** <Assets at fair market value, grants paid, fiscal year,
role. Foundation assets are not personal wealth.> [n]

**Giving elsewhere.** <Named gifts and donor-roll ranges, with year and source.
"A <name> is listed" until a second fact ties the listing to the prospect.> [n]

**Stated by the prospect.** <Questionnaire answers or contact-report statements
from the organization's records; officer and date. Self-reported.>

**Political giving.** <Totals by cycle; a signal of discretionary cash only.> [n]

## Giving to our organization
<Household total, outstanding commitments, main designations and years, from
the organization's records. Say who provided them.>

## Relationship to our organization
<Degrees, volunteer roles, board or council service, event attendance, relatives
who are alumni.>

## Capacity
**Major gift capacity: <low> to <high> (tier), over 3-5 years, based on <indicators>.**
Confidence: <higher | moderate | lower>, because <reason>.
Upside not in the range: <private company, family wealth, unconfirmed holdings>, because <basis>.
Unknown: <what, and what would settle it>.
Organization's rating: <if provided, with date>. <Note any gap of more than one tier.>

## Sources
1. <Record or publication>, <date>. <URL>
```

Notes:

- Bullet-point prose is the norm here, not tables. Keep it short.
- Every dollar figure carries its record and date in the sentence.
- Rate the person, not the household: a spouse's separate assets and family
  wealth go on the upside line.
- Some shops add a rating form at the end for the reviewing officer: their
  rating, the date, and a signature line. Add it when the user asks.
- If the user wants a batch (a unit's pool), produce one evaluation per person
  and a summary table: name, tier and range, main driver, confidence, officer
  rating if any, gap flag.
