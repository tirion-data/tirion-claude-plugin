# Template: in-depth briefing / prospect research profile (PRP)

For a new top prospect, a portfolio review, or a solicitation plan. Usually 4 to
10 pages. The Research Summary is the most-read section: put the conclusion there.

The section order below follows the convention of university research shops:
identity and ratings, summary, background, affiliations, family, wealth,
capacity, giving to us, relationship to us, contact history, strategy, sources.

```markdown
# Prospect research profile: <Full name>
**Confidential: for internal use by <organization> only**
Prepared <YYYY-MM-DD> by <researcher or team>, with AI assistance. Reviewed by <name>, <date>.
Requested by <officer>. Purpose: <qualify / plan cultivation / prepare solicitation / portfolio review>.
Facts current as of <date>. Supersedes the profile dated <date>, if any.

| | |
|---|---|
| Prospect manager | <assigned officer, from the organization's records> |
| Research rating | <tier label, low to high, this profile> |
| Organization's rating | <officer or overall rating and its date, from the organization's records; "none" if not provided> |

If the two ratings differ by more than one tier, say so in the summary. Do not
merge them. The gap is information.

3. Their relationship with us and the most recent contact, from the organization's records.
4. Strongest affinity and warm path: why us, who opens the door.
5. Flags: sensitivities, data gaps, disagreement with the organization's rating.
6. Confidence and key unknowns: what would change the conclusion.>
```

Notes:

- Name the record and date inside the sentence as well as in the Sources list:
  "Per SEC Form 4 filed 2026-03-04, Jordan Avery held 392,000 shares" [3].
  A reader should not need the footnote to know where a fact came from.
- File name convention many shops use: `Lastname, Firstname M. - PRP - YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Drop a wealth subsection that has nothing sourced. Do not write "None found";
  write what is unknown in "What we do not know".
- Keep capacity separate from the philanthropy and relationship sections. The ask
  range is where they meet.
- "Why they give" explains motivation, not means. Keep it short even in a
  full PRP, especially when evidence is thin; do not repeat the biography.
- "Giving to our organization", "Contact history" and the organization's rating
  come from the user, not from public records. Ask for them. Never invent them.
- For an organization or a foundation as the prospect, replace Identity, Family
  and Career with: mission, leadership and board (see `get_board_roster`), assets
  and grants by year, giving priorities, application process and deadlines.
