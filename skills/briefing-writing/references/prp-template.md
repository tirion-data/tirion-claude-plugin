# Template: in-depth briefing / prospect research profile (PRP)

For a new top prospect, a portfolio review, or a solicitation plan. Usually 4 to
10 pages. The Research Summary is the most-read section: put the conclusion there.

```markdown
# Prospect research profile: <Full name>
**Confidential: for internal use**
Prepared <YYYY-MM-DD> by <name or team> for <requesting officer>.
Purpose: <qualify / plan cultivation / prepare solicitation / portfolio review>.
Facts current as of <date>.

## Research summary
<5 parts, 4-6 sentences:
1. Capacity conclusion: tier label, range, main driver.
2. Evidence basis: the records it rests on.
3. Strongest affinity and linkage: why us, who opens the door.
4. Flags: sensitivities, data gaps, disagreement with any officer rating.
5. Confidence and key unknowns: what would change the conclusion.>

**Recommended next step:** <who, what, by when>.
**Ask range:** <low> to <high>, for <purpose>, timing <when and why>.

|---|---|

| Age range | <if public, e.g., "born 1961" from a proxy> [n] |
| Spouse or partner | <name, only if public and relevant> [n] |
| How identity was confirmed | <anchors: employer, city, filing> |

## Biography
<Standard (about 250-word) sourced bio. See bio-writing.>

1. <Record or publication>, <date>. <URL>
```

Notes:

- File name convention many shops use: `Lastname, Firstname M. - PRP - YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Drop a wealth subsection that has nothing sourced. Do not write "None found";
  write what is unknown in "What we do not know".
- Keep capacity separate from the philanthropy and relationship sections. The ask
  range is where they meet.
- For an organization or a foundation as the prospect, replace Identity and
  Career with: mission, leadership and board (see `get_board_roster`), assets and
  grants by year, giving priorities, application process and deadlines.
