## Worked example of clustering

Candidates in a metro, after qualifying: 11 people. Two are pending asks, four are current donors, five are prospects.

1. Put the two asks on Day 1 morning and Day 2 morning, so each can have a follow-up before departure.
2. Map all 11 to areas (from the organization's records or their linked property): 5 in the north suburbs, 4 downtown, 2 on the west side.
3. Day 1 north: one ask + two donors + one prospect. Day 2 downtown: one ask + two donors + one prospect. The west-side two become alternates or a half-day if a slot opens.
4. Fill the flexible slot with the best alternate once the confirmed meetings are known.

## Meeting purpose vocabulary

- **Discovery:** first meeting; learn interests, qualify capacity and affinity. No ask.
- **Cultivation:** deepen involvement; share a project; invite to an event or a tour.
- **Solicitation (ask):** a specific amount for a specific purpose, agreed in advance with the team.
- **Stewardship:** thank, report on impact, maintain the relationship.
- **Congratulatory:** after a liquidity or career event; acknowledge it; no ask.
