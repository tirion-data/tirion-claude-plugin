# Liquidity events: where to find who was paid, how much and when

All filings are on SEC EDGAR (sec.gov/edgar). EDGAR full-text search (sec.gov/edgar/search) finds a name across filings since 2001.

## Public company acquired (merger)

| Question | Filing | Where in the filing |
|---|---|---|
| Deal announced, terms | 8-K, Item 1.01 (entry into a material agreement), press release as Exhibit 99.1 | Price per share, consideration, expected closing |
| Who among insiders gets what | DEFM14A (definitive merger proxy) or PREM14A | "Interests of the Company's Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger"; "Golden Parachute Compensation" table; treatment of options, RSUs and PSUs |
| Tender offer version | Schedule TO (acquirer), Schedule 14D-9 (target's recommendation) | 14D-9 Item 3, "Past Contacts, Transactions, Negotiations and Agreements", lists insider holdings and payments |
| Large holders | Latest DEF 14A, "Security Ownership of Certain Beneficial Owners and Management"; Schedules 13D and 13G | 5%+ holders, directors and officers, footnotes naming trusts and family entities |
| Closed | 8-K, Item 2.01 (completion of acquisition or disposition) | Closing date |
| Stock consideration | S-4 registration statement (acquirer) | Exchange ratio; any lock-up |

## IPO

| Question | Filing | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-IPO owners | S-1 (and amendments), final prospectus 424(b)(4) | "Principal Stockholders" or "Principal and Selling Stockholders" |
| Who sold in the offering | Same | "Selling Stockholders" columns: shares offered |
| Lock-up | Same | "Shares Eligible for Future Sale"; "Underwriting". Commonly 180 days; early-release terms vary |
| After listing | Form 3 (initial insider holdings), Form 4 (changes), Form 144 (proposed sales) | |

## Insider sales and holdings

Form 4 is filed within two business days of a transaction by officers, directors and 10% owners. Transaction codes that matter:

| Code | Meaning | Read as |
|---|---|---|
| S | Open-market or private sale | Cash received (gross) |
| P | Open-market purchase | Buying; a confidence and liquidity signal |
| M | Exercise or conversion of a derivative (options) | Often paired with S on the same day |
| F | Shares withheld to pay tax or exercise price | Not a sale by choice; not cash to the person |
| A | Grant or award | Compensation; not cash |
| G | Gift | Often to a family foundation, donor-advised fund or family trust; an inclination signal |
| J | Other | Read the footnote |

Footnotes often state that sales were made under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan (a pre-arranged plan). A 10b5-1 plan implies a steady schedule of future sales.

Form 144: notice of a proposed sale of restricted or control securities by an affiliate, filed with the SEC around the time of the sale order. It shows intent, not a completed sale.

Value current holdings at a recent closing price, and give its date. Value each past transaction at its own event-date price, not today's price. When a person leaves the company, Form 4 reporting stops; later holdings cannot be confirmed from filings.

## Private company sold

- If the acquirer is public and the deal is material, the 8-K or the next 10-Q/10-K may disclose the price.
- Press releases, local business journals and trade press name founders and investors.
- State corporate filings name officers, not owners.
- Do not estimate a founder's percentage without a source. State "price not disclosed" or "founder's share not public".

## Sizing rules

- Gross value = shares x price per share. Before tax, before any debt secured by the shares.
- Long-term capital gains tax and state tax reduce net proceeds materially. Do not compute a net figure; say "before tax".
- Unvested awards: the proxy states whether they accelerate, convert to acquirer awards, or are cancelled.
- Earn-outs and escrow holdbacks: part of the price paid later, and sometimes never.
- Stock consideration: the value moves with the acquirer's share price and may be locked up.
- Report a range and its basis. Never a single figure.

## Timing and the contact

| Event | Contact |
|---|---|
| Deal announced, not closed | Monitor. A light note if there is an existing relationship |
| Deal closed | Congratulatory note or call from the closest relationship |
| IPO priced | Congratulations. Note the lock-up date |
| Lock-up expiry | Watch for Form 4 and Form 144 filings |
| Large Form 4 sale | Update the capacity rating. Stewardship or cultivation contact as the relationship allows |

A liquidity event raises capacity. It does not create affinity. Rank the beneficiaries with the prioritizing-prospects rubric.
