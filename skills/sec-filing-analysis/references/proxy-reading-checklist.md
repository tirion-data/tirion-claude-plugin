# Reading a proxy (DEF 14A) and deal documents: checklist

The DEF 14A is the definitive proxy statement a public company files before
its annual meeting. It is the best single source for an officer's or
director's pay, holdings and outside roles. Find it on EDGAR under the
company's filings, form type "DEF 14A". A merger vote uses "DEFM14A".

## 1. Summary Compensation Table (named executive officers)

Covers the CEO, CFO and the next three highest-paid officers, for the last
three fiscal years. A smaller reporting company covers fewer officers (the CEO
and the next two) and two years.

- [ ] Salary and Bonus: cash.
- [ ] Stock Awards and Option Awards: **grant-date fair value** under
      accounting rules (ASC 718). This is not cash and not what the person
      will realize. It vests over years.
- [ ] Non-Equity Incentive Plan Compensation: cash bonus tied to targets.
- [ ] Change in Pension Value and Nonqualified Deferred Compensation.
- [ ] All Other Compensation: perquisites (aircraft use, security, club
      dues). Aircraft and security are lifestyle signals.
- [ ] Total. Report it, but say what share is cash and what is equity.
- [ ] "Pay versus Performance" table (in proxies filed since 2023): "compensation actually
      paid" is closer to realized value than the total. Cite it by name.

## 2. Equity tables

- [ ] Outstanding Equity Awards at Fiscal Year-End: unvested stock and
      unexercised options, with exercise prices and expiry dates. This is the
      pipeline of future liquidity.
- [ ] Option Exercises and Stock Vested: what vested or was exercised in the
      year, and its value on the vesting or exercise date. Close to realized.
- [ ] Potential Payments upon Termination or Change in Control: the sums due
      if the person leaves or the company is sold. Key before a deal close.

## 3. Director Compensation Table

- [ ] Fees earned in cash plus stock awards. Directors of large companies
      typically receive a mix of cash retainer and annual stock grant.

## 4. Security Ownership of Certain Beneficial Owners and Management

- [ ] Shares beneficially owned by each director and named officer, and by
      5%+ holders, as of a record date near the filing.
- [ ] "Beneficial ownership" includes options exercisable within 60 days and
      shares held by trusts, spouses and entities the person controls.
- [ ] Read every footnote: trust names, family partnerships, pledged shares
      (a pledge means the stock backs a loan; the person's liquid equity is
      lower), and disclaimers.
- [ ] Value the holding at a stated price and date.

## 5. Director and officer biographies

- [ ] Age (a proxy is one of few public sources of age), career history,
      education, other public boards.
- [ ] Nonprofit boards and trusteeships named in the bio. These are affinity
      signals; confirm them in 990s via `nonprofit-990-analysis`.
- [ ] Prior companies. A prior exit may be a past liquidity event worth a
      separate EDGAR search.

## 6. Related-party transactions ("Certain Relationships and Related Transactions")

- [ ] Leases, loans, consulting fees or purchases between the company and an
      insider or family member. These show other business interests and
      family members in the business.
- [ ] Charitable contributions by the company to organizations where a
      director serves. Shows the director's causes.

## 7. Other items

- [ ] Hedging and pledging policy.
- [ ] Stock ownership guidelines (a CEO required to hold, for example, 6x
      salary in stock).
- [ ] Shareholder proposals and say-on-pay results: not wealth, skip unless
      relevant.

## S-1 and 424B prospectus (IPO)

- [ ] "Principal and Selling Stockholders": pre-IPO holders, shares before
      and after the offering, shares sold in the offering. Sold shares are
      realized at the IPO price less underwriting discount.
- [ ] Footnotes name the funds, trusts and entities each person holds through.
- [ ] "Shares Eligible for Future Sale" and "Underwriting": lock-up length
      (commonly 180 days) and any early-release terms. Compute the expiry date.
- [ ] "Executive Compensation" and "Management": same use as the proxy.
- [ ] The 424B4 is the final prospectus with the price. Use it for values.

## Merger documents

- [ ] 8-K Item 1.01 (merger agreement signed) and Item 2.01 (deal closed).
      The closing date is the liquidity date.
- [ ] DEFM14A or S-4: per-share consideration (cash, stock, or mix), and the
      "Interests of Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger" section,
      which often tabulates what each insider receives. Cite it directly.
- [ ] For an acquisition of a private company by a public one, the acquirer's
      8-K or press release may name the price but not the founders' shares.
      Say the founder's share is unknown unless a filing shows it.

## Citation form

"<Company> DEF 14A, filed <date>, Summary Compensation Table, FY<year>" with
the EDGAR URL.
