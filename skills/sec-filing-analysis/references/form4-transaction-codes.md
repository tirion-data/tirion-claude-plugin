# Form 3, 4 and 5 transaction codes: what each means for liquidity

Forms 3, 4 and 5 are filed under Section 16 of the Securities Exchange Act by
officers, directors and 10%+ holders of a public company ("insiders").

- **Form 3**: initial statement of holdings, filed within 10 days of becoming
  an insider. No transactions; it is a starting balance.
- **Form 4**: a change in holdings, filed within 2 business days of the
  transaction. Most research uses this form.
- **Form 5**: an annual catch-up for transactions exempt from or deferred
  from Form 4 (some gifts historically, small acquisitions). Due within 45
  days after the company's fiscal year end.

Since February 27, 2023 bona fide gifts (code G) must be reported on Form 4
within 2 business days, not deferred to Form 5. Gifts made before that date
may appear only on a Form 5.

## The codes

| Code | Meaning | Cash to the person? | How to value | What it signals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P** | Open-market or private purchase | No (cash out) | Reported price x shares | Confidence in the company; also proof of liquid cash available to invest. |
| **S** | Open-market or private sale | **Yes** | Reported price x shares (gross, pre-tax) | A liquidity event. The strongest timing signal on the form. |
| **A** | Grant or award from the issuer (RSUs, restricted stock, options) | No | Event-date close x shares for stock; spread for options | Pay, often unvested. Paper wealth that builds over years. |
| **M** | Exercise or conversion of a derivative exempt under Rule 16b-3 (usually an employee option) | No by itself | (Market price minus exercise price) x shares | Often paired with a same-day S (cashless exercise) or F. Read the pair. |
| **X** | Exercise of an in-the-money or at-the-money derivative (not 16b-3 exempt) | No by itself | Same as M | Same as M. Check for a paired S. |
| **C** | Conversion of a derivative (for example preferred to common) | No | Event-date close x common shares | Often at an IPO or deal close. A change of form, not cash. |
| **F** | Payment of exercise price or tax by delivering or withholding shares | No | Event-date close x shares withheld | Tax withholding on vesting. Not a sale decision. |
| **G** | Bona fide gift | No | Event-date close x shares (no price is reported) | Read the footnote for the recipient: family trust (estate planning), foundation or DAF (philanthropy), named charity. |
| **D** | Disposition to the issuer (buyback, forfeiture, merger exchange) | Sometimes | Reported price if any | In a merger, D lines show shares exchanged for cash or acquirer stock. |
| **U** | Disposition in a tender of shares in a change-of-control transaction | Yes, if the tender is for cash | Tender price x shares | A deal closing by tender offer. Read with the Schedule 14D-9. |
| **J** | Other acquisition or disposition (described in a footnote) | Depends | Read the footnote | Often distributions from a fund or partnership to its partners. |
| **W** | Acquisition or disposition by will or the laws of descent | No | Event-date close | Inheritance, or an estate distribution. A life event. |
| **I** | Discretionary transaction under an employee benefit plan | Rare | Reported price | Plan fund moves. Minor. |
| **L** | Small acquisition (under Rule 16a-6) | No | Reported price | Minor. |
| **Z** | Deposit into or withdrawal from a voting trust | No | n/a | Governance, not wealth. |
| **V** | Voluntary early report of a transaction | Depends | By the underlying code | Look at the underlying transaction. |
| **K** | Equity swap or similar | Depends | Read the footnote | Hedging. Can mean the person has locked in value. |

Also check column 2A "Deemed execution date" and the Rule 10b5-1(c) checkbox
(required on Forms 4 and 5 filed on or after April 1, 2023). A 10b5-1 trade
follows a plan adopted earlier. For officers and directors, a plan adopted
since February 27, 2023 has a cooling-off period of at least 90 days (up to
120 days) before the first trade.

## Reading common patterns

- **M + S same day, same share count**: cashless exercise-and-sell. Cash is
  (sale price minus exercise price) x shares, before tax.
- **M + F same day**: exercise with shares withheld to pay exercise price or
  tax. The person keeps the rest. No cash realized.
- **Vesting: A earlier, then F on the vest date**: RSUs vested; shares were
  withheld for tax. The remaining shares are now held and sellable.
- **Series of S lines over weeks, all 10b5-1**: a scheduled selling program.
  Sum over the period, and say whether the plan appears to continue.
- **G to "Family Foundation" or a DAF sponsor**: a charitable gift of
  appreciated stock. Report the value at the event-date close and the
  recipient. A strong philanthropic signal and proof of a giving vehicle.
- **J from a venture or PE fund**: a distribution in kind from a fund to its
  partners. The partner now holds liquid stock.
- **D lines at a merger close**: shares exchanged. Look for the 8-K Item 2.01
  and the merger proxy for the per-share consideration.

## Direct and indirect ownership

Column 6 is "D" (direct) or "I" (indirect). Column 7 names the nature of
indirect ownership: "By Trust", "By Spouse", "By GRAT", "By LLC", "By Family
LP". A GRAT (grantor retained annuity trust) is an estate-planning vehicle
that passes growth to heirs. Count indirect holdings as family capacity, and
report any footnote that disclaims beneficial ownership "except to the extent
of pecuniary interest".

## Valuation rules

1. Use the price on the Form 4 line when there is one.
2. When the line has no price (A, G, C, W, often F), use the closing price on
   the transaction date from a public quote source. State the source and date.
3. For a weighted-average price, use it; note the range from the footnote.
4. Options: value at (event-date or current price minus exercise price) x
   shares, only if positive, and only for vested options. Show unvested apart.
5. Always report sales as gross proceeds. Taxes and cost basis are unknown.
6. Report holdings "as of" the last filing date. Section 16 reporting ends
   when the person stops being an insider; later sales are not reported.
