| Mechanism | What the filing shows | Is it cash now | When it becomes cash | Common pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sale | Shares sold, price, date and holder | Cash proceeds if the sale settled | At settlement | Gross proceeds are not net worth or after-tax cash. |
| Offering proceeds | Proposed or final proceeds to the issuer or selling holders | Only after payment to the named recipient | At offering settlement | The issuer's proceeds do not belong to its founder. |
| Deal consideration: cash | Cash price, eligible holdings and conditions | Only if paid | At closing or a later payment date under the agreement | An announced deal may not close. |
| Deal consideration: stock | Exchange ratio and shares received | No | A later permitted sale and settlement | Stock value is not cash received. |
| Deal consideration: earnout | Possible additional payment and performance conditions | Usually no | When conditions are met and payment occurs; check cash or stock terms | The maximum is not a guaranteed payment. |
| Award | Granted shares, units or options and their terms | Usually no | Cash settlement or a later sale, subject to the terms | Grant-date accounting value is not cash pay. |
| Vesting | Conditions lapse on an award | Not necessarily | Cash settlement or a later sale of delivered shares | Vesting and payment can have different dates. |
| Option exercise | Options exercised, strike price and shares acquired | Exercise alone does not produce sale proceeds | A separate sale settles | Exercise cost and taxes reduce proceeds from an exercise-and-sale. |
| Lock-up expiry | A contractual sale restriction ends | No | A permitted sale settles | Expiry neither requires nor proves a sale. |
| Compensation | Salary, bonus, equity and other pay for a period | Depends on the component and payment status | On the stated payment or settlement date | Total compensation can include noncash award values. |
| Distribution | Cash or property distributed to a holder | Only the cash portion if paid | Cash payment, or a later sale of distributed property | Not every distribution is cash or personal income. |
| Gift or transfer | Assets move to another holder or recipient | Usually no sale proceeds | Only a separately documented cash payment or later sale | A transfer is not necessarily a sale or a charitable gift. |
